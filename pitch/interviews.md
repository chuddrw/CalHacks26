# Interviews with other teams

> **What this file is for:** notes from talking to other Cal Hacks teams about how their AI agents step on each other. One real quote opens the pitch, and Anthropic's judging asks whether we talked to the people who have the problem. D runs these tonight (20 minutes, while teams are integrating) and again on Saturday.

## How to ask

- Walk up when a team looks like it's merging or debugging, not in the middle of a crunch.
- "Quick question for a project, 2 minutes: are you each running AI coding agents? Has one ever broken a teammate's code without anyone noticing until later?"
- Follow-ups: What happened? How long did it take to find? How did you find it? Did git warn you?
- Ask before quoting: "Can we quote that in our pitch? First name and team, or anonymous?"
